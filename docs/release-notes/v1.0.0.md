First public release.

Take a photo, crop it to a standard ID or passport size, arrange copies on one
sheet, and print it — or save a PDF to print somewhere else. Everything happens
on the device: no account, no upload, no internet needed.

## Which file do I download?

| Your phone | File |
| --- | --- |
| Almost every phone made since 2017 | `app-arm64-v8a-release.apk` |
| Older or budget devices | `app-armeabi-v7a-release.apk` |
| Emulators | `app-x86_64-release.apk` |

If you are not sure, take **arm64-v8a**. Android refuses to install the wrong
one, so nothing breaks if you guess. You will need to allow installing from
outside the Play Store the first time.

## What is in it

- Four numbered steps: add, adjust, layout, print
- Photo sizes: 35×45 mm, 2×2 in, 35×35 mm, 4×6 in, or a custom size in mm
- Pinch to zoom and drag to frame, with a passport guide overlay and an eye line
- Rotate photos that came out sideways
- 4, 6, 8 copies or fill the sheet, on A4, Letter or 4×6 in paper
- Several people on one sheet, shared out evenly
- Drag any photo to rearrange it, with alignment guides that snap like Word's
- Tap a photo to swap who is in that slot
- Print through the printer your phone already knows about, or export a PDF

## Printing accuracy

The layout is calculated in millimetres, and the PDF is written with the same
numbers rather than a rescaled screenshot. Photos are embedded at 300 DPI, with
a 2 mm gap between them to cut along. A 35 × 45 mm photo comes out 35 × 45 mm.

## Known limits

- One sheet at a time — asking for more copies than fit caps the number rather
  than starting a second page
- Nothing is saved when the app closes, on purpose
- iOS is supported by the code but has not been built or tested
